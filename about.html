About AQUAE Labs & AQUAE Impact

Bringing Ecological Science and Natural Capital Finance Together

Developing scientific methodologies and technology infrastructure to measure, assess and recognise the ecological value of natural ecosystems.

The Ecological Recognition Gap

Connecting Field Observations with Responsible Valuation

AQUAE Labs works at the intersection of ecological science, ecosystem assessment, natural capital valuation, environmental markets and digital technology. Its work focuses on developing scientific methodologies and technology infrastructure to measure, assess and recognise the ecological value of natural ecosystems.

Natural ecosystems are living, productive and regenerative systems. They provide essential ecosystem services, including biomass production, oxygen generation, water purification, cooling effects, soil development, water retention and biodiversity conservation. These ecological functions support environmental stability, ecosystem resilience and human wellbeing. However, their contributions are often not adequately recognised within conventional economic and financial systems.

AQUAE Impact aims to address this gap through scientific assessment, ecological accounting and the development of the AQUAE Labs Ecosystems Conservation Index.

By connecting field observations and ecological data with valuation methodologies, digital records and risk-management approaches, AQUAE Impact seeks to support more informed decisions about conservation, restoration and natural capital.

Our Vision

A future in which the ecological value of natural ecosystems is scientifically recognised, transparently measured and meaningfully integrated into conservation, restoration and responsible economic decision-making.

Our Mission

To develop and advance scientific methodologies and digital infrastructure for measuring ecosystem services, assessing ecological performance and supporting the responsible valuation and recognition of natural capital.

Our Purpose

To make ecological value more visible, assessable and traceable, helping support the conservation and restoration of natural ecosystems.

Foundational Standards

Our Principles

Five core principles guide AQUAE Labs and AQUAE Impact across all ecosystem assessments, valuation methodologies and environmental asset records.

Principle 01

Scientific Integrity

Ground ecosystem assessments in field-based observations, relevant data and documented scientific evidence.

Principle 02

Transparency

Support clear documentation of assessment methods, valuation assumptions and environmental asset records.

Principle 03

Traceability

Connect ecological evidence with structured records of environmental assets and related transactions.

Principle 04

Responsible Valuation

Present ecosystem value with appropriate context, limitations and supporting evidence.

Principle 05

Conservation

Support the long-term protection, restoration and responsible management of natural ecosystems.

Our Commitment

Making Ecosystem Value Visible, Assessable and Traceable

AQUAE Labs is committed to connecting ecological science, environmental assessment, digital infrastructure and risk management through an integrated approach to natural capital assessment and valuation.

Through the AQUAE Labs Ecosystem Conservation Index (ALCI), ecosystem assessment and valuation methodologies, the developing insured ALCI asset offering, AQUAEChain, analog forestry initiatives and its intellectual property development, AQUAE aims to strengthen the understanding and recognition of ecosystem value.

The work begins with real landscapes and the ecological functions they support. Through documented assessment, transparent valuation and appropriate technology, AQUAE Labs seeks to strengthen the connection between conservation, ecological evidence and responsible environmental markets.

The long-term objective is to contribute to the recognition of natural ecosystems as essential components of environmental and economic resilience while supporting conservation, restoration and regenerative sustainability initiatives.

Grounded in Real Landscapes

From Belipola Arboretum in Sri Lanka’s central highlands to developing conservation and restoration initiatives, every assessment starts with empirical field observations.
