AQUAE Labs & AQUAE Impact • Biophilic Telemetry

Making Nature’s Value Visible

AQUAE Labs is developing scientific methods and technology infrastructure to assess, value and track the services provided by natural ecosystems. Through the AQUAE Labs Ecosystems Conservation Index (ALCI), the company aims to connect ecological assessment with natural capital valuation, environmental markets and risk management.

From forests and wetlands to biodiversity-rich restoration landscapes, AQUAE Impact works to make the value of ecosystems more visible, measurable and accessible to organisations seeking evidence-based approaches to conservation and regenerative sustainability.

Living Field Laboratory • Sri Lanka Central Highlands

Grounded in Real Landscapes: Belipola Arboretum

Established in the early 1980s by systems ecologist Dr. F. Ranil Senanayake on formerly degraded tea plantation land, Belipola Arboretum in Sri Lanka is recognised as the world’s first Analog Forest and serves as the primary field benchmark for the ALCI scientific methodology.

Field monitoring across Belipola’s mapped sectors (Zones A–U, nursery, hydrological streams and mature climax canopy) demonstrates how one hectare of mature analog forest generates approximately 435,511 liters of oxygen, 16.59 million liters of water purification capacity, and microclimate cooling equivalent to 1.87 million kWh annually.

Transforming Conservation From a Cost Into Measurable Value

The AQUAE Labs Ecosystems Conservation Index (ALCI) quantifies, verifies, and values ecosystem services through standardized field Monitoring, Recording, and Verification (MRV) across seven domains: biomass, oxygen production, water purification, microclimate cooling, living soil organic matter (SOM), water retention, and biodiversity.

Belipola Arboretum Empirical Benchmark (1 Hectare / Year) Produces ~435,511 liters of oxygen, 16.59 million liters of water purification capacity, and microclimate cooling equivalent to 1.87 million kWh of energy.

KEY METHODOLOGICAL INSIGHTS FIELD MRV & 33-YEAR FRAMEWORK

Scientific Integrity & Smart-Contract Traceability

Each ALCI credit reflects actual ecological outputs from real, geospatially mapped land parcels classified under IUCN Habitat Classification Guidelines (v3.1). Sites undergo annual and seasonal field re-monitoring over 5-year MRV validation cycles within a 33-year ecological maintenance framework.
