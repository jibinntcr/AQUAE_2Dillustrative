SERVICES & PROJECT DEVELOPMENT

Scientific and Technical Services for Natural Capital Assessment

Helping organisations understand ecosystem condition, assess ecological value and develop evidence-based approaches to conservation, restoration and environmental markets.

SERVICES

Comprehensive Natural Capital & Ecological Services

AQUAE Impact’s services are intended to help organisations understand ecosystem condition, assess ecological value and develop evidence-based approaches to conservation, restoration and environmental markets.

Ecosystem Assessment and MRV

Field-based assessment and monitoring support the collection and organisation of relevant ecological information. MRV processes help document ecosystem conditions, indicators and outcomes using appropriate methods.

Ecosystem Valuation

AQUAE Impact works to assess the services provided by ecosystems and translate relevant ecological indicators into structured valuation reports. Valuations depend on the underlying data, methodology, assumptions and scope of assessment.

Landscape Valuation Reports

Landscape Valuation Reports are intended to present the ecological characteristics, ecosystem services and assessed value of a defined landscape. Reports may support project planning, conservation strategy, institutional engagement and further evaluation of potential environmental market opportunities.

Analog Forestry and Restoration Support

AQUAE Impact’s work includes support for ecosystem restoration approaches that draw on ecological principles and the development of biodiverse landscapes. Project scope depends on site conditions, restoration objectives and the expertise required.

ALCI Asset Development

Ecosystem assessments and valuation reports can support ALCI Credit development, subject to project eligibility, the applicable methodology and verification requirements. The acceptance of credits in the market depends on project-specific documentation and buyer requirements.

Insurance-Related Risk Structuring

AQUAE Impact’s developing insurance framework is intended to connect qualifying environmental assets with appropriate risk assessment and potential insurance protection. Any actual coverage remains subject to insurer underwriting and the applicable policy terms.

Digital Ecosystem Records

AQUAEChain is being developed to support the management and traceability of environmental credit data, ecosystem assessment records and related documentation.

Education and Capacity Building

AQUAE Impact supports knowledge-sharing through educational initiatives, training and webinars focused on ecosystem services, ecological assessment, natural capital valuation and environmental markets.

PROJECT DEVELOPMENT

From Landscape Assessment to Conservation Value

AQUAE’s project-development approach begins with the ecological characteristics of a site and the outcomes a project seeks to support. The objective is to establish an evidence-based foundation for conservation, restoration and, where appropriate, environmental credit development.

Project Development Process

1. Baseline

Site Identification and Baseline Assessment

Identify the landscape and assess its ecological condition, existing land use, relevant ecosystem functions and project objectives.

2. Field Data

Ecological Monitoring and Data Collection

Gather the field information required to understand ecosystem characteristics and establish an appropriate baseline.

3. MRV Analysis

MRV and Ecological Analysis

Organise and evaluate the collected information using relevant monitoring, reporting and verification procedures.

4. Valuation

Landscape Valuation

Assess the ecosystem services supported by the site and prepare valuation documentation based on the applicable methodology and available evidence.

5 & 6. Planning

Restoration and Conservation Planning

Identify appropriate measures for ecological restoration, biodiversity conservation and long-term landscape stewardship.

7. Credit Review

ALCI Credit Assessment

Evaluate whether the documented ecosystem services and project outcomes may be suitable for ALCI Credit development, subject to eligibility, methodology and verification requirements.

8. Risk Review

Insurance Assessment

Assess whether identified project risks may qualify for insurance coverage, subject to underwriting approval, policy terms, exclusions and coverage limits.

9. Market Engagement

Digital Documentation and Market Engagement

Maintain digital records where the necessary infrastructure is available, and identify relevant institutional partners and environmental markets based on project eligibility and market requirements.

Project eligibility, valuation, credit issuance, insurance and market access are determined separately and should not be assumed from completion of a site assessment alone.
